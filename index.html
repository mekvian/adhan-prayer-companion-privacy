1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Adhan Prayer Companion ("the App"), developed by MEKVIAN, handles your information. The App provides accurate Islamic prayer time calculations, Qibla direction compass, Adhan audio reminders, daily Dhikr/Tasbih counters, and spiritual reflection tools.

We are deeply committed to respecting your personal privacy. The App does not require user registration or account creation, and user preferences are retained directly on your device.

2. Location Information & Ephemeral Processing

Key Guarantee: Your location is processed locally on your device in real-time solely to calculate astronomical prayer times and determine the Qibla compass bearing towards the Kaaba (Mecca). It is never sent to our servers or stored remotely.

The App requests device location permissions (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION):

  • Prayer Times Calculation: To determine sunrise, noon, and astronomical sun angles corresponding to your geographic coordinates.
  • Qibla Direction: To calculate the exact geodesic azimuth pointing from your current position towards Mecca.

Location data is processed ephemerally in memory and is not transmitted to developer servers or sold to data brokers. You may also manually select your city without granting GPS permissions.

3. Advertising & Monetization

The App is monetized through advertising to support ongoing development and maintenance. Advertisements are selected to remain contextual, ethical, and respectful of the spiritual nature of the App.

Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may collect standard device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID (AAID), in accordance with Google Play Developer Policy. This data is used solely for frequency capping, ad fraud prevention, and aggregated reporting. No sensitive or spiritual activity data is ever shared with advertising networks.

4. Device Permissions Used

  • Location: Required for local prayer time and Qibla compass calculations.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to deliver Adhan call to prayer reminders and countdown alerts.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to play Adhan alerts at exact prayer minutes even in Doze/standby mode.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Required for smooth audio playback of adhan audio selections.

5. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

All network communications (such as retrieving updated calculation tables or advertising requests) utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption in transit. Local preferences and settings are safely contained within the App's sandboxed private storage on your device.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

How to Delete Your Data:
Because the App operates without user accounts, all preferences and cached times are stored locally on your device.

Option A — Instant On-Device Deletion:

  • Open your device's Settings > Apps > Adhan Prayer Companion.
  • Tap Storage & Cache and select Clear Storage (Clear Data). All local settings, prayer records, and cached calculations will be permanently deleted immediately.
  • Uninstalling the App permanently removes all associated application files from your device.

Option B — Requesting Support Deletion:

If you have contacted us via email for support or feedback, you can request full deletion of any correspondence and associated records by emailing mekvian@proton.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request - Adhan Prayer Companion". All verified requests will be permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13.
